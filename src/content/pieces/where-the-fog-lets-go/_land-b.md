I had been standing in the same place for an hour when the far bank
first showed. Not all at once: a darker gray inside the gray, then a
line where the water stopped and the trees began. I made this frame in
the first minute and did not move the tripod for the next ten.

The exposure is long enough that the surface went to glass. That was
not the plan. The plan was to hold the highlights in the fog, and the
shutter that did it happened to still the water too. I have learned to
let the second thing happen.

It is the frame the whole morning was waiting for, and the one I would
print from that day. _Fixture story — invented prose standing in for
the photographer's own, never adopted as content._
