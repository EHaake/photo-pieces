---
title: The headlands
description: >-
  The stretch of coast above the cove, walked in every season since
  the first fog piece. Fixture place — invented prose, placeholder
  frames.
---

I keep going back because the weather never repeats. The fog either
holds or it doesn't, and either way the trail is the same forty
minutes from the car. _Fixture writing — invented prose standing in
for the photographer's own, never adopted as content._

Below the writing the page gathers every outing there, oldest first,
as the pieces were published.
